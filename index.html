Study method

  1. Read the relevant section in your guidebook.
  2. Close the book and explain the material in your own words.
  3. Use flashcards to test what you actually remember.
  4. Write down missed questions and revisit them later.
  5. Ask a qualified leader to clarify anything uncertain.

Leadership Schools

Preparation hubs for JLS, SLS, and ALS. Confirm dates, prerequisites, packing lists, and eligibility with your chain of command.

🧭
Junior

JLS — Junior Leadership School

Focus on learning the basics, following instructions, teamwork, accountability, and being a reliable teammate.

Preparation checklist
  • Review your current guidebook.
  • Practice drill and uniform preparation as directed by your unit.
  • Work on clear communication and listening.
  • Confirm authorized packing requirements.
  • Ask your leaders what to expect at your specific school.
🛡️
Intermediate

SLS — Senior Leadership School

Build on the fundamentals with initiative, accountability, peer leadership, and sound judgment.

Preparation checklist
  • Review relevant guidebook topics.
  • Prepare examples of teamwork and problem-solving.
  • Practice giving clear instructions.
  • Think through respectful conflict resolution.
  • Confirm current school-specific guidance with your unit.
⭐
Advanced

ALS — Advanced Leadership School

Develop a broader perspective on mentorship, planning, communication, and serving the people you lead.

Preparation checklist
  • Study relevant advanced guidebook material.
  • Prepare real examples of mentoring and initiative.
  • Practice planning and presenting an idea.
  • Reflect on how to improve your unit.
  • Use your authorized school materials and current instructions.
School descriptions on this page are general preparation suggestions, not official curricula, prerequisites, selection criteria, or promises of admission.

Leadership reminders

  • Lead by example before asking others to follow.
  • Listen carefully before making assumptions.
  • Give credit and accept responsibility.
  • Ask for help when a problem exceeds your role or experience.
  • Take feedback seriously without treating mistakes as your identity.
This tool does not send submissions to anyone. It is a local reflection worksheet, not a live advice submission service or a substitute for help from a trusted adult or qualified leader.

About YM360

Built to help Young Marines learn, lead, and serve.

Our purpose

YM360 is an independent project intended to make learning resources, preparation tools, and leadership development easier to navigate.

The goal is to support growth from the first steps in the program through more advanced leadership responsibilities.

Our standards

  • Respect official program instructions.
  • Separate official requirements from independent advice.
  • Be honest about the source of questions and materials.
  • Protect personal information and restricted resources.
  • Verify changing requirements with authorized leaders.

Disclaimer and content use

YM360 is not an official Young Marines, U.S. Marine Corps, or Marine Corps League website and is not endorsed by those organizations. Names and acronyms are used to describe the intended subject matter. This site does not establish official eligibility, promotion requirements, awards criteria, school prerequisites, or uniform regulations.

Only publish manuals, logos, photographs, and other materials when you have the right or permission to do so. Do not imply that independent material is an official publication.
